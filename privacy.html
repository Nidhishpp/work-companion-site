Summary

The short version. The full policy below is what counts.

Your data stays with you

Work Companion runs on your computer. It has no servers and no accounts. ChaiStack never receives your data.

No tracking

No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or advertising, in the app or on this website.

Calendar is read-only

Google Calendar is optional and read-only. Its data is never sent to AI services and never written to disk by the app.

AI is your choice

AI features are optional. Requests go straight from your computer to the provider you pick, with your own key or sign-in.

Secrets are locked away

Keys and sign-in tokens are kept encrypted in your operating system’s keychain, never in plaintext.

You are in control

Delete your log folder, sign out or uninstall at any time. There is nothing held by ChaiStack to delete.

Who we are

Work Companion is a desktop app by ChaiStack that runs on your own computer. It has no servers and no user accounts, and the developer does not receive, collect or store any of your data.

ChaiStack publishes Work Companion and this website. For privacy questions, data requests or grievances, write to privacy@chaistack.in.

What stays on your computer

  • Work log: plain markdown files in a folder you choose.
  • Settings: a settings file in the app’s profile folder.
  • Secrets (AI API keys and Google sign-in tokens): stored encrypted in your operating system’s keychain (Windows Credential Manager, macOS Keychain) or, on Linux, encrypted with your keyring. They are never stored in plaintext.
  • Activity: the app checks how many seconds have passed since your last keyboard or mouse input, to tell whether you’re still working after hours. It never records what you type or click.
  • Character models: 3D models you load are read from your disk and never uploaded.

The app has no analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or advertising.

Google Calendar

Connecting Google Calendar is optional. If you sign in, Work Companion requests one scope, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly (read-only), and nothing else.

Data accessed

  • Your calendar list: each calendar’s ID and name, so you can pick which ones to follow. Your Google account’s email address is read from your primary calendar’s ID, to show which account is connected.
  • Today’s events on the calendars you pick: title, start and end time, whether the event is cancelled, your own response (to skip events you declined), and the event’s video meeting link. If an event has no video link, its location and description are scanned for a meeting link; only the link is kept.

The app reads nothing else from your Google account. It does not create aggregated or anonymized data from your Google data.

How the data is used

Only for features you see in the app: showing today’s schedule, alerting you a few minutes before a meeting with a Join button, holding back break reminders while you’re in a meeting, and asking for a short note when a meeting ends. The app never creates, edits or deletes calendar data.

Sharing and transfer

Google Calendar data goes directly from Google to your computer and is not shared with anyone: not with the developer (ChaiStack has no servers and never receives it), not with AI providers, advertisers, data brokers or any other third party. It is never sold.

Protection

All requests to Google use HTTPS (TLS). Sign-in uses Google’s OAuth with PKCE through your own browser, so the app never sees your Google password. The sign-in tokens and account email are stored encrypted in your operating system’s keychain (on Linux, encrypted with your keyring; without a keyring they are kept in memory only for that session). Event data is kept in the app’s memory only and is never written to disk by the app.

Retention and deletion

Event data is replaced at every sync (every 5 minutes) and discarded when you sign out or quit the app. If you write a note when a meeting ends, the note is saved in your own work log with the meeting’s title in front of it; that log is a file on your computer that you control and can edit or delete at any time. Signing out in the app revokes its access with Google and deletes the stored tokens. You can also remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Because the developer never receives your data, there is nothing held by ChaiStack to delete.

Limited Use and AI

Work Companion’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The use of raw or derived user data received from Google Workspace APIs will adhere to the Google User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app never sends the data it reads from Google to any AI service, and Google user data is never used to develop, improve or train AI or machine learning models. The one exception you control: a meeting note you choose to save becomes part of your own work log, with the meeting title in front of it, and if you later use an AI feature on your log, that entry is sent like any other log entry.

AI providers

AI features are optional. If you set up a provider (for example Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini, OpenRouter or a custom server), the app sends the log entries needed for the feature you use, such as a standup summary or a question about your log, directly from your computer to that provider with your own key or sign-in. The app never adds Google Calendar data to these requests; only meeting notes you saved to your log, with their meeting titles, can be part of them. That provider’s privacy policy applies to those requests. With a local Ollama model, nothing leaves your computer.

If you use Sign in with ChatGPT, you sign in to OpenAI in your own browser. The app keeps the sign-in tokens and your account email in your keychain, and requests are billed to your ChatGPT plan under OpenAI’s terms. Removing the sign-in in the app forgets those tokens.

ChaiStack does not see, relay or store any of these requests.

Microsoft Store

If you install Work Companion from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft handles the download, installation and updates under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. The developer may see the aggregated, anonymous install and usage reports Microsoft provides to all Store publishers, but no personal data and none of your work data.

This website

This website (workcompanion.chaistack.in) is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other sites: no external fonts, scripts, CDNs or embeds.

The only thing it stores is your light or dark theme choice, in your browser’s localStorage. It never leaves your browser. See the cookie and storage policy.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process visitors’ IP addresses in its server logs, for example for security, under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. ChaiStack does not receive those logs.

If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply, and keep them only as long as needed for that.

Your rights

Privacy laws give you rights over your personal data. Because ChaiStack never receives any data from the app, we hold nothing about you unless you email us. Most requests are therefore met by controls you already have on your own computer:

  • See your data: open your log folder. Each day is a plain markdown file you can read in any text editor.
  • Correct it: edit those files in any text editor.
  • Delete it: delete the log folder or single day files. Remove saved AI keys and sign-ins in the app’s settings.
  • Take it with you: the log is already plain markdown; copy the folder wherever you like.
  • Stop calendar access: sign out of Google Calendar in the app (this revokes access and deletes the tokens), or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Remove everything: uninstall the app, then delete the log folder and the app’s profile folder if you want them gone too.

For data held by Google, Microsoft or an AI provider you use, contact that company; its own policy applies.

EU and UK (GDPR and UK GDPR)

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your personal data, to data portability, and to object to or restrict processing. You also have the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority where you live or work. For any personal data ChaiStack does hold (for example an email you send us), write to privacy@chaistack.in. Our legal basis for handling your email is our legitimate interest in answering you.

India (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023)

As a data principal you have the right to information about your personal data, to correct, complete, update and erase it, to grievance redressal, and to nominate someone to use your rights if you die or cannot act. To make a request or raise a grievance, write to privacy@chaistack.in. We aim to respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

California (CCPA/CPRA)

ChaiStack does not sell or share personal information, as California law defines those terms, and does not process sensitive personal information. You have the right to know, delete and correct personal information, and you will not be treated differently for using these rights. Write to privacy@chaistack.in.

International transfers

ChaiStack does not transfer your data anywhere, because it never receives it. When you choose to connect a service, such as Google Calendar or an AI provider, data moves between your computer and that service, which may process it in other countries under its own policy.

Children

Work Companion is a productivity tool and is not directed at children under 13.

It is also not intended for children as defined in India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (under 18). ChaiStack does not knowingly process children’s personal data.

Security

Secrets are kept in your operating system’s keychain, network requests use HTTPS (TLS), and sign-ins use OAuth with PKCE in your own browser. See the security page for details and how to report a vulnerability.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: privacy@chaistack.in. For help with the app: support@chaistack.in.

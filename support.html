Quick start

  1. Install and open

    Get the installer from the Download page. The app lives in your tray or menu bar.

  2. Set up your day

    In the dashboard, set work hours, pick a character and choose your reminders.

  3. Capture notes

    Press Ctrl+Shift+Space (Cmd+Shift+Space on macOS) to add to today’s log.

Troubleshooting

The quick capture hotkey does nothing

Another app may already use Ctrl+Shift+Space (for example an input-method switcher). Close that app or change its shortcut, then restart Work Companion. On macOS, allow the app in System Settings if it asks for permission.

Google sign-in does not finish

Sign-in opens in your default browser and returns to the app on your own computer. Make sure the browser can reach the local address it redirects to, that you approved read-only calendar access, and try again. You can disconnect and reconnect from the app’s settings.

“Test connection” fails for AI

Check the provider, the model name and the base URL, and that your API key is valid and has credit. For Ollama or LM Studio, make sure the local server is running and the model is downloaded.

Where do my logs live?

One markdown file per day, in the log folder shown in the app’s settings. You can change the folder, and open or back up the files with any editor.

A reminder did not show up

Only one bubble shows at a time, and low-priority reminders wait during meetings, focus blocks and Do Not Disturb. Check that the reminder is turned on and the schedule is correct.

The character is too big or in the way

Change its size, corner and behaviour (walk in for notifications or stay on screen) in the dashboard.

Last updated: 27 November 2026

This website

We aim for this website to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. It uses plain HTML with headings, landmarks and a skip link, works with the keyboard, follows your system’s light or dark setting, and uses system fonts that scale with your browser.

The app

  • Keyboard quick capture: a global hotkey (default Ctrl+Shift+Space, changeable in Settings) opens a small box to add a log entry. Enter saves and Esc closes it.
  • Plain-text log: your work log is plain markdown, so you can read and edit it with any editor or screen reader you already use.
  • Without the character: you can mute or hide the animated character. When it is muted, reminders appear as your operating system’s own notifications.
  • Sound: sound effects can be switched off, and their volume adjusted, in Settings.

Known limitations

  • The 3D character is decorative. Its animations carry no information you can’t get another way, but they are not described to screen readers.
  • The dashboard turns off its animations and transitions when the operating system’s reduce-motion setting is on. The 3D character does not follow that setting yet; hiding the character, or choosing the idle movement setting, reduces motion on screen.
  • The app has not had a formal accessibility audit.

Feedback

If something is hard to use, please tell us at support@chaistack.in. We aim to reply within 5 working days.

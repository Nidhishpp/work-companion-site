Full list

Every installer includes THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md next to the app. It lists every bundled component, about 350 in the Windows and macOS app and 15 in the Linux app, with its version, its licence and the full licence texts. It is generated from the app’s dependency lists for each release. Almost all components are under MIT, Apache-2.0 or both; a few use BSD, ISC, Zlib, Unicode-3.0, MPL-2.0 or CDLA-Permissive-2.0. The Linux app also ships Electron’s and Chromium’s own licence files.

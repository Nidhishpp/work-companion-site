Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Work Companion is a desktop app that runs on your own computer. It has no servers and no user accounts, and the developer does not receive, collect or store any of your data.

What stays on your computer

  • Work log: plain markdown files in a folder you choose.
  • Settings: a settings file in the app's profile folder.
  • Secrets (AI API keys and Google sign-in tokens): stored encrypted in your operating system's keychain (Windows Credential Manager, macOS Keychain) or, on Linux, encrypted with your keyring. They are never stored in plaintext.
  • Activity: the app checks how many seconds have passed since your last keyboard or mouse input, to tell whether you're still working after hours. It never records what you type or click.
  • Character models: 3D models you load are read from your disk and never uploaded.

The app has no analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or advertising.

Google Calendar

Connecting Google Calendar is optional. If you sign in, Work Companion requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly scope and uses it only to:

  • list your calendars so you can pick which ones to follow;
  • read today's events (title, start and end time, and your response) to show them, alert you before meetings, and ask for a note when a meeting ends.
  • if you set up an AI provider and click Write with AI on the Standup card, include the start times and titles of today's events in that one request, so the summary can mention your meetings.

Event data is fetched directly from Google to your computer and kept in memory. It is never sent to the developer, never sold, never used for advertising and never used to train AI models. The only other place it goes is the standup request described above, which happens only when you ask for it, goes directly from your computer to the AI provider you configured with your own key, and contains only event start times and titles. With a local Ollama model it never leaves your computer. The app never creates, edits or deletes calendar data. Signing out in the app revokes its access with Google and deletes the stored tokens. You can also remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Work Companion's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

AI providers

AI features are optional. If you set up a provider (for example Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini, OpenRouter or a custom server), the app sends the log entries needed for the feature you use, such as a standup summary or a question about your log, directly from your computer to that provider with your own key or sign-in. That provider's privacy policy applies to those requests. With a local Ollama model, nothing leaves your computer.

Microsoft Store

If you install Work Companion from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft handles the download, installation and updates under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. The developer may see the aggregated, anonymous install and usage reports Microsoft provides to all Store publishers, but no personal data and none of your work data.

Children

Work Companion is a productivity tool and is not directed at children under 13.
